Fluffy French Toast


Description

The secret ingredient in this fluffy French toast recipe: all-purpose flour! Flour binds the liquids together,
which creates a more traditional “batter” and helps prevent soggy results.
This extra ingredient ensures the French toast is crispy on the outside, but soft and fluffy on the inside.

Ingredients

  • Flour: This fluffy French toast batter starts with ¼ cup all-purpose flour.
  • Milk adds moisture and richness to the batter. You can substitute half-and-half, cream, or your favorite alternative milk if you like.
  • Of course, you’ll need eggs for French toast! Three eggs bind the batter together and give it a rich texture.
  • A tablespoon of white sugar lends welcome sweetness to this decadent breakfast recipe.
  • A teaspoon of vanilla extract adds complexity and takes the flavor up a notch.
  • Ground cinnamon lends coziness and warmth. .
  • A pinch of salt enhances the flavors of the other ingredients, but it won’t make your French toast taste salty:
  • You’ll need 12 slices of your favorite thick-sliced bread. Good choices include brioche, sourdough, French bread, or challah.

    • Steps

    1. Slowly whisk the milk into the flour, then whisk in the remaining ingredients.
    2. Saturate the bread slices in the batter.
    3. Cook the French toast on a lightly oiled griddle or pan until golden on both sides.
